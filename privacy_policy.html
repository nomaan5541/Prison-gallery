Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026 • Package: com.prismgallery.qwvpmr

Core Privacy Guarantee: Prism Gallery is a 100% offline, privacy-first media gallery. We do not operate servers, we do not collect, store, or transmit your photos, videos, or personal data, and we do not use third-party tracking or advertising SDKs.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Prism Gallery (“we”, “our”, or “the app”), developed by Virus Boss (@Noman5541), handles your data. We believe your memories, photos, and videos belong solely to you. The application is architected from the ground up to operate completely on-device without internet permissions.

2. Information Collection and Use

We collect ZERO personal data.

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account logins.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: No usage logs, crash telemetry, or user interaction trackers are bundled with this application.
  • No Advertising Networks: There are no advertisements, ad trackers, or advertising identifiers (AAID) used in this app.
  • No Cloud Sync or Server Storage: Your media files remain exclusively on your physical Android device.

3. Device Permissions Explained

To function as a full-featured media gallery, video player, and editor, Prism Gallery requests the following permissions strictly for on-device operations:

Photos and Videos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)
Used exclusively to scan, display, categorize, and organize your local photos and videos into your timeline, albums, and folder structures. Files are processed in memory and never leave your phone.
Media Location (ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION)
Allows reading EXIF location tags stored in your photos to display GPS details in photo information dialogs. Prism Gallery provides a built-in Secure Sharing feature that can strip all GPS and EXIF data before you share any media with external apps.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)
Optional permission used solely to deliver local on-device "Daily Memories" notifications (e.g. photos taken on this day in past years). This schedule is handled entirely by Android's local AlarmManager without any push notification servers.
Biometrics & Fingerprint (USE_BIOMETRIC, USE_FINGERPRINT)
Used on-device through Android's secure BiometricPrompt API to lock and unlock your private vault or the app itself. Biometric verification is handled entirely by your device hardware; biometric credentials never touch app code.
Set Wallpaper (SET_WALLPAPER)
Used only when you explicitly select "Set as wallpaper" for a photo to apply it to your device's home screen or lock screen.

4. In-App Photo Editor and Vault

  • Photo Editor: All image enhancements, cropping, filter adjustments, and markup happen on-device using Android Bitmap canvas and hardware acceleration. Edited files are saved to your local device storage.
  • Private Vault: Media moved to your secure vault is stored in private internal app directories to prevent other gallery applications from indexing them. Your data never leaves your device.

5. Third-Party Services and Code Libraries

Prism Gallery uses only trusted, official Android Jetpack open-source libraries (Compose, Media3 ExoPlayer, Coil for image caching, ExifInterface) to render and play media locally. None of these components transmit telemetry or communicate with external analytics servers.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Because Prism Gallery does not collect any personal information from any user, it is fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies. The app is safe for users of all ages.
